# 📚 ResearchMate AI
### AI Research Paper Reading Assistant

ResearchMate AI is an AI-powered learning application designed to help undergraduate students understand academic research papers more easily. It uses Google's Gemini API to simplify technical concepts, summarize research papers, generate quizzes, and create interactive flashcards from uploaded PDF documents.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Research papers often contain complex terminology, technical methodologies, mathematical concepts, and lengthy explanations. Undergraduate students may find it difficult to identify key findings, understand research methods, and revise important concepts efficiently.

ResearchMate AI aims to make academic research more accessible through AI-assisted explanations and interactive learning tools.

## 💡 Proposed Solution

ResearchMate AI allows students to upload a research paper in PDF format and use AI-powered tools to understand and revise its content. It extracts readable text from the PDF and uses Gemini to generate learning materials based on the extracted content.

## ✨ Key Features

- **PDF Upload:** Upload academic research papers in PDF format.
- **Research Paper Summary:** Generate structured summaries covering the research problem, objectives, methodology, results, and limitations.
- **Simple Explanations:** Explain technical concepts in beginner-friendly English.
